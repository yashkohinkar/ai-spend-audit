## How to run these (interview script)

**Recruiting (10–15 min conversations, 3 people):**
- Cold-DM founders/engineering leads on X who post about their dev stack or AI tool spend.
- Post in your college network / a founder Slack / Indie Hackers: "Building a tiny free tool that audits AI tool spend — can I show you a prototype for 10 min and hear what's wrong with it?"
- Friends running side projects with paid AI tools count. Three humans, real conversations.

**Outreach DM template:**
> Hey [name] — I'm building a free no-login tool that shows startups where they're
> overpaying on AI tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, etc.). 10-minute call?
> I'll screen-share, you tear it apart. No pitch, just want your honest reaction.
> Would [day] [time] work?

**Question script (don't send it; ask live, let them ramble):**
1. "Walk me through the last time your team's AI tool bill went up. What did you do?"
2. "Which AI tools does your team pay for right now, and who decided?" (get specifics — plans, seats, rough $)
3. "When the bill lands each month, what's your gut — 'fine' or 'this feels too high'? What stops you from doing anything about it?"
4. "If a free tool told you 'drop Cursor Business → Pro, save $X/mo' with a cited source, would you trust it? What would make you trust it? What would make you not?"
5. "Show me the audit (screen-share the live tool). What's missing? What's wrong? What would make you share the result with your cofounder / finance?"

**Capture:** Take notes live, but write up the direct quotes within an hour while they're fresh. The surprising thing is usually in questions 3 and 5 — the workaround they currently use, or the thing they assumed the tool would do that it doesn't.

**Turn each interview into a design change.** If an interview didn't change anything about the product, it didn't count — go talk to someone else.
