## FAQ — 5 real Q&As

**Q: Do I need to log in or connect a billing account?**
A: No. You type in the tools, plans, and what you pay — we audit against current official vendor pricing. No login, no OAuth, no card. Your numbers stay in your browser until you choose to save a report.

**Q: Where do your prices come from?**
A: Every plan price is verified against the vendor's own pricing page (cursor.com, claude.com, openai.com, etc.) with the date we checked, all listed in PRICING_DATA.md. We re-verify before each update. If a price is wrong, that's on us — and it's traceable.

**Q: How do you decide what's "overspending"?**
A: Four checks per tool: is the plan overkill for your team size (e.g., a Team plan for two people), is there a cheaper plan from the same vendor that fits, is there a substantially cheaper alternative tool for your use case, and are you paying retail API rates when you'd qualify for volume/committed-use discounts. Each recommendation comes with a one-line, finance-readable reason.

**Q: Why do you show savings before asking for my email?**
A: Because you should see the value before we ask for anything. The full audit and a shareable public link are free with no email. We only ask for your email if you want to save the report or get notified when new optimizations apply to your stack.

**Q: Is my data public if I share my audit?**
A: Only if you click "Share." The public link shows tool names, plans, and savings numbers only — your email and company name are stripped. Anyone with the link can see those numbers; nobody can see who you are.
